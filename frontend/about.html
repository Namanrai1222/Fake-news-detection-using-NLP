How it works.

A transparent text classifier for article triage, with an optional separate fact-check lookup.

A

Clean and split

We combine ISOT with nonoverlapping WELFake articles, correct a verified label reversal, remove duplicate normalized text, and keep matching headlines within one split. Training, validation, calibration, and test sets stay separate. Preprocessing is identical during training and prediction.

B

Fit a compact model

TF–IDF word pairs feed a logistic regression classifier. We compare feature sizes and weakly labeled general-news examples on validation data, then calibrate probabilities on a separate article split.

C

Show what influenced it

The analyzer displays influential terms. These explain the model's estimate, not the factual basis of a claim. A Google Fact Check lookup runs only when the server has an API key; an optional local Ollama model can summarize the output.

D

Test the boundary

Held-out ISOT and new WELFake articles test article classification. AG News and BBC samples check ordinary news false positives by topic; LIAR claims expose a different task. AG News and BBC carry topic labels, not independent truth verdicts; LIAR claims are not merged into article training.

Use it as a lead.

For consequential decisions, read the original reporting, check dates and sources, and look for corroboration.
